Logo Authorisation Platform

Report on an activity using the Decade logo

Organisations authorised to use the logo of the Decade of Action for Cryospheric Sciences (2025–2034) report on their activity: a progress update while it runs, and a final report once it ends. The information is used by the UNESCO Secretariat to follow the activities carrying the logo and to compile the reporting of the Decade.

You need the request reference and the reporting code sent with your authorisation. If you do not have them, write to cryosphere@unesco.org.
